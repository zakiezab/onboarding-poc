User flow · Onboarding POC

How a new hire gets onboarded by an AI voice guide

From a unique HR invite link to a completed first morning — narrated end to end by a speaking orb, with a live voice-conversation branch available the whole way through.

Product: Mobizinc Onboarding Example hire: Tom John Example department: Creative & Multimedia 5 stages · 8 walkthrough beats · 1 branch
1

Entry

Invite link opens, already personalised

Every hire gets a unique link from HR. Opening it resolves straight to their name and department — nothing is typed in at this stage.

“You’re invited, Tom John.” This link is yours — it already knows you’re joining Creative & Multimedia.
2

Identity

Sign in with Microsoft

A single button, styled after Microsoft’s own sign-in convention. No credentials are collected — it’s a stand-in for the SSO check a real deployment would run.

taps “Login using Microsoft”
3

Gate

Good morning, and a deliberate first tap

Browsers block audio until a person acts, so the walkthrough can’t just autoplay. This screen exists to be that one required gesture.

“Good morning, Tom John.” Your accounts are ready. Your guide will walk you through the rest — about fifteen minutes, with sound.
taps “Start onboarding”
4

The walkthrough

The orb speaks, eight beats in order

Each beat plays as one continuous, natural-sounding line — captioned live underneath, word by word, as it’s spoken.

Welcome› Your accounts› What we do› How we work› Your department› Your people› Your first week› Getting help

Say that again

Replays the current beat from the start.

Continue

Cuts off the guide mid-sentence if needed, then plays the next beat.

Follow-up chips

Beat-specific questions, e.g. “Who else is on the team?” — the guide answers, grounded in the HR docs, then the beat’s own controls return.

Your department

The one beat that’s different per hire — reads the actual Creative & Multimedia team brief aloud.

01Your accounts → Accounts and tools 02How we work → How Mobizinc works 03Your department → Your department 04Your first week → Your first week 05Getting help → Where to get help

Optional, any beat

Live conversation

One tap on the mic hands the orb over to a real-time, two-way voice call — the same guide, but now actually listening. Speaking over it cuts it off mid-sentence (barge-in), the way interrupting a person would.

tap mic→ live call opens→ ask anything, interrupt freely→ tap mic again→ back to the exact beat it left
5

End of walkthrough

“Getting help” finishes the sequence

After the eighth beat, Continue relabels itself Finished and disables — there’s nowhere further to advance to. The progress rail reads 100%.

On screen the whole time (stages 4–5)

The orbReacts to whoever is talking — the guide during the walkthrough, either side during a live call. No 3D avatar is shown; the orb is the entire visual presence.
Progress railFive checklist items, filling in as the matching beat finishes — visible at every stage, never just at the end.
Live captionThe line currently being spoken, highlighted word by word, capped to four lines with auto-scroll.
Reflects the current build of the onboarding POC. The invite link and department (Tom John / Creative & Multimedia) are hardcoded for this proof of concept — a shipped version would decode both from the link itself.
