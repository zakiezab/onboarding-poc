# Service Desk

Source: Service Desk Manager Onboarding Action Plan. Name of the individual
removed and reframed as a team overview per the strip-names call — the
source is a single new hire's 90-day plan, not a department roster.

<!-- spoken -->
Service Desk owns Tier 1 support for Mobiz's clients — the ServiceNow
queues, SLA performance, and the staffing behind both. The core skill the
role is built around is operational judgment: reading queue health and
acting on it before a client notices a problem, not just clearing tickets
as they land.

You're joining an existing Tier 1 and Tier 2 team. There's no single named
project here — the work is continuous operations rather than a project with
a start and end, so what you'll actually be doing is running and improving
the queues that are already live: incident, request, problem, and change
tickets moving through their normal lifecycle, with a Major Incident
process for anything serious.

Your first week is about understanding what's already running before you
touch anything: get your ServiceNow access confirmed, review the current
assignment groups, SLA definitions, and open ticket backlog for every
supported client, and meet the Tier 1 and Tier 2 staff directly to hear
their daily friction points firsthand. By week two you start shadowing the
daily triage cadence, and by week four you begin taking ownership of
routine SLA and staffing decisions, with a peer delivery lead reviewing
before anything goes live.

## Reference

Not read aloud, but available when the new hire asks a question.

- **Never** deploy an SLA, routing, or escalation change the same day it's
  drafted — minimum 24-hour review window, since a bad routing change
  surfaces as a missed ticket within hours, not weeks.
- **OLA vs SLA**: know the difference — an Operational Level Agreement issue
  between Service Desk and NOC/SOC/engineering is often the actual cause of
  an SLA breach, not the desk itself.
- **Milestones**: Day 30 — navigating ServiceNow unassisted, full queue/SLA
  baseline complete. Day 45 — first corrected routing issue and SLA policy
  change, reviewed and accepted. Day 60 — owns weekly SLA and staffing
  review; has built or refined a Performance Analytics dashboard. Day 90 —
  closed the loop on a Problem Management root-cause reduction.
- **Reporting**: Performance Analytics dashboards for SLA compliance, CSAT,
  and backlog aging — built or refined as part of the role, not just
  consumed.
