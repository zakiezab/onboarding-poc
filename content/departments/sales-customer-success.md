# Sales & Customer Success

Source: Mobiz Sales and Customer Success Onboarding Guide (MENA team).

<!-- spoken -->
Sales and Customer Success run the full customer relationship as one
journey in two towers. Presales owns the first half — qualifying a lead,
running technical discovery, and getting a statement of work signed.
Customer Success owns the second half — onboarding a client after the deal
closes, driving adoption, and handling renewal and expansion. A delivery
team sits between the two towers, running the actual project once it's
sold. Each tower has its own leads — a Presales Lead and a CS Practice
Lead — with a Regional Technical Director over both.

Mobiz's MENA client base spans government, banking, telecom, energy, and
industrials, delivered against Microsoft's funding programs where they
apply. You won't be quoting deals in week one. Your first days are about
access — Dynamics 365, ServiceNow, SharePoint, and Slack — and getting
oriented in the company overview materials. In week one, you'll sit in on a
live customer call or a QBR if one's scheduled, and review a few closed
opportunities to see how the sales process actually plays out. By day
ninety, you should be able to move a deal through the CRM correctly on your
own, or run a full QBR cycle if you're on the Customer Success side.

Three rules shape every conversation with a client: scope before price,
anchor on outcomes not activity, and funding is a lever, never the deal.

## Reference

Not read aloud, but available when the new hire asks a question.

- **Systems**: Dynamics 365 CRM ("mobizsales") is the system of record for
  every lead and opportunity. ServiceNow is where delivery runs once a deal
  closes. SharePoint hosts client folders and the sales enablement library.
- **The customer lifecycle**: six stages — intake & qualify, discovery &
  solution, estimate & SOW, delivery & governance, onboard & adopt, renew &
  expand — with two formal handoff gates (Presales to PMO, PMO to CS).
- **Hygiene rules**: no signed SOW filed, no Close Won. A Lead only becomes
  an Opportunity with a named buyer, a stated problem, and a plausible SOW.
- **Customer Success**: account health is tracked across four pillars —
  relationship, technical, commercial, and consumption — giving each account
  a Red/Amber/Green status reviewed quarterly.
- **Escalation channels**: #sales for general collaboration, #salessupport
  for CRM/pipeline questions (an internal assistant called Mia can pull live
  pipeline data there), #presales-engineering for technical scoping.
