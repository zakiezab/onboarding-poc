# Creative & Multimedia

Source: Onboarding Plan — Creative Multimedia Designer. Name of the incoming
designer removed per the strip-names call; the Reporting Manager's name is
kept since it's explicitly a management-relationship fact in the source, not
a new hire's personal subject matter.

<!-- spoken -->
Creative and Multimedia produces Mobizinc's client-facing and internal
creative output — static and motion graphics, branding, and marketing
collateral. The team reports up through Zakie Zabar, and works closely with
marketing, development, and account teams whenever a piece needs to land
inside a client-facing product or campaign.

The team's day-to-day workflow runs brief intake, then concept, then
review, then delivery, all built around Mobizinc's existing brand
guidelines — palette, typography, and voice. There's always a live motion
graphics or branding project in progress that a new designer shadows before
picking up their own work.

A new designer's first week is entirely about immersion before output: HR
and IT setup on day one, then two full days going deep on the brand
guidelines and the existing asset library, shadowing a live project, and
meeting the marketing, development, and account collaborators they'll work
with. By day four, they're given a small, low-stakes creative task using
real brand guidelines, paired with a senior designer for guidance — the
first thing they actually produce, however small. From there, ownership
grows in thirty-day phases: small supervised assets first, then a
small-to-medium project with lighter supervision, then independent
ownership of standard creative requests by day ninety.

## Reference

Not read aloud, but available when the new hire asks a question.

- **Tools**: Adobe Creative Cloud, Figma, and the team's project management
  tool — provisioned on day one.
- **Evaluation criteria** at the 90-day review: craft and quality, tool
  proficiency, ownership and reliability, collaboration, and initiative and
  growth.
- **Milestone check-ins**: end of week 4 (informal, progress against early
  goals), end of week 8 (progress review, flag support needed), and the
  formal Day 90 probationary assessment — which includes a self-assessment
  and a short portfolio of the onboarding work itself.
- **Probation outcomes**: confirmed in role, probation extended with a
  defined improvement plan, or employment not confirmed.
